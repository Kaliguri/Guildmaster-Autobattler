bug.svg — "bug" (outline). Picked by Max 2026-08-22 over the game-icons candidates: the line style
reads better at 26 px inside a button than a solid silhouette does.

`stroke="currentColor"` is rewritten to `#ffffff`: Unity does not resolve currentColor, and the
interface tints these glyphs.

NOTE: the rest of our game art (run-mode icons, guild emblems) is game-icons.net — solid
silhouettes, CC BY 3.0. Tabler is used only in the service row of the main menu, where the whole
row shares one line style. Do not mix the two sets inside one row.
